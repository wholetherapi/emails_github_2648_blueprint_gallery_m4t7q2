## Overview

Supplier Portal Vendor Onboarding is a staged draft for a supplier-facing
workflow. It will cover registration, compliance document collection, risk
review and first-order enablement.

## Design highlights

- **Progressive registration** — suppliers can start with basic profile data
  and complete compliance steps over several sessions.
- **Document checklist** — required certificates and insurance documents are
  tracked per supplier category.
- **Risk review hand-off** — borderline suppliers are routed to a compliance
  reviewer before activation.
- **First-order enablement** — supplier accounts are unlocked for ordering only
  after approval.
